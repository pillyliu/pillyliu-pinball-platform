PinProf Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 17, 2026

PinProf ("the App") is provided by Peter Liu ("I," "me," "my"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App processes and how it is used.

1. Information Collected

The App is designed to work without account sign-in and does not require you to provide personal information to use core features.

The App may process:

  • League and game content files downloaded from the app data source (standings, stats, targets, machine info, rulesheets, images)
  • Information you choose to save in the App, such as practice history, goals, GameRoom records, settings, and local notes
  • Optional Cloud Sync account and sync data, if you choose to sign in and use Cloud Sync
  • Anonymous product analytics, unless you turn analytics off in Settings
  • Current location or venue search text when you choose to search for nearby venues
  • Basic technical request data handled by your device/network stack when fetching remote content (for example, IP address at the network level)

No sensitive personal information is intentionally collected through in-app forms.

2. How Information Is Used

Data is used to:

  • Display league standings, stats, targets, and machine library content
  • Save your app data, preferences, and progress on your device
  • Sync supported text data across your signed-in devices when you choose to use Cloud Sync
  • Find nearby venues when you choose to use current-location venue search
  • Understand which screens and features are used so the App can be improved and debugged
  • Cache content locally for performance and offline-friendly behavior
  • Refresh content metadata and updates

3. Data Storage

The App stores downloaded content files and user-created app data on your device to support local-first use.

  • Cache data can be cleared by uninstalling/reinstalling the App
  • Cloud Sync is optional and only applies when you choose to sign in and use it
  • Cloud Sync is intended for supported text data. Photos, videos, camera images, OCR images, and local media files are not synced in the current Cloud Sync version.

4. Analytics, Tracking, and Advertising

The App may use PostHog for product analytics. Analytics are used to understand and debug the App, not for advertising. Analytics can be turned off in Settings.

  • No third-party advertising SDKs are used in the App
  • No cross-app tracking is used for targeted advertising
  • Analytics events are explicit named product events, such as screens viewed, buttons or dropdowns used, feature sections opened, app lifecycle events, and time spent on a screen or rulesheet
  • Analytics events do not intentionally include typed values, score values, OCR text, OCR images, IFPA or PRPA IDs, email addresses, auth codes, names, notes, import files, media files, or file paths
  • Session replay, wireframe replay, and screen recording analytics are not enabled for the 3.6.2 release

5. Optional Cloud Sync

If you choose to use Cloud Sync, the App may use Supabase to operate account sign-in and sync. Cloud Sync may process your account email, sign-in code flow, supported synced text data, device/sync metadata, and related support diagnostics.

Cloud Sync is optional. The App remains local-first and usable without Cloud Sync.

6. Third-Party Content and Links

The App may display or link to third-party resources, including external websites and YouTube-hosted content.

Venue search is powered by Pinball Map. If you use current-location venue search or enter a venue/address search, PinProf sends that location or search text through a secure PinProf.com server endpoint and then to Pinball Map to return nearby venue results. The server endpoint uses request bodies rather than URL parameters and is designed not to write typed addresses or precise coordinates to application logs. New imported venues retain their Pinball Map venue ID for later lineup refreshes instead of requiring the original search text.

When you open third-party content, their privacy policies and terms apply.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. Personal information is not knowingly collected from children.

8. Data Sharing

PinProf will never sell user data. PinProf will not share user data with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties for their own use. Trusted service providers may process limited data only to operate PinProf features, including hosting/content delivery, optional Cloud Sync through Supabase, and product analytics through PostHog. PinProf's hosting provider and Pinball Map process location or venue-search information only when you use a venue lookup or another feature that explicitly requests nearby venue evidence.

9. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect content delivery and app operation. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

10. Your Choices

  • Turn off product analytics in App Settings
  • Choose not to use Cloud Sync, or stop using Cloud Sync
  • Request account or synced-data deletion by contacting support
  • Stop all data processing by uninstalling the App
  • Avoid opening third-party links if you do not want their services to process data

PinProf and PinProf Field Guide Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

PinProf and PinProf Field Guide are provided by Peter Liu. This policy covers both apps on iOS and Android. Some features are available only in one app; those differences are explained below.

1. PinProf Field Guide

Field Guide works without an account. Photos, recognized game names and scores, field notes, audio notes, saved venue information, progress, and settings are stored on your device. Photo recognition and score reading run on your device. Field Guide does not upload your photos or audio notes to PinProf for recognition or storage.

Field Guide does not use PostHog product analytics or the optional Supabase Cloud Sync feature described below for the original PinProf app.

2. Permissions and photo metadata

  • Camera: used when you take a field photo or use an available AR photo feature.
  • Photos: used to browse and import photos you select, read available capture dates and location metadata, or save a photo to your library. The scope of access depends on the permission you grant and your operating system.
  • Location: used to suggest nearby Pinball Map venues for a field photo or venue lookup. Imported photo location metadata may also be used to suggest a venue. This can include precise coordinates when available. Background location access is not requested.
  • Microphone: used when you choose to record an audio note. Listening to narration or existing audio does not require microphone access.

You can change these permissions in your device settings. Denying a permission limits the related feature; you can still browse the guide and use features that do not need that permission. Location permission is optional. Less accurate or unavailable location may prevent an automatic venue suggestion.

3. The original PinProf app

The original PinProf app stores downloaded league and game content, practice history, goals, GameRoom records, local notes, and settings on your device. Core features do not require account sign-in.

If you choose Cloud Sync, Supabase processes your account email, sign-in information, supported synced text data, and device or sync metadata. Photos, videos, camera images, OCR images, and local media files are not part of the current Cloud Sync feature.

The original PinProf app may use PostHog for product analytics. You can turn analytics off in its settings. Events describe product use, such as screens viewed, controls used, app lifecycle events, and time spent on a screen. They are not intended to include typed text, scores, OCR text or images, IFPA or PRPA IDs, email addresses, sign-in codes, names, notes, import files, media files, or file paths. Session replay and screen recording analytics are not enabled.

4. Venue lookups and network requests

Venue features are powered by Pinball Map. When you use a nearby-venue feature or search by address, the app sends coordinates or search text to a secure PinProf.com endpoint. That endpoint requests results from Pinball Map. Coordinates may come from your device location or a photo's location metadata. The photo itself is not sent with that lookup.

The venue service caches lookup parameters and results. Cache anchors can include precise coordinates or address search text, without an app account identifier attached. Results normally refresh after 48 hours, but that refresh interval is not a deletion deadline: cache entries can remain until replaced or removed during maintenance. The service is designed not to write typed addresses or precise coordinates to application logs.

Hosting and content services receive technical request information such as an IP address, request time, requested resource, and network or device information. Security controls may use a derived network identifier to limit requests. Technical information may be retained in server logs or security records to operate and protect the services.

5. Third-party content and links

The apps download game data, rulesheets, images, and other reference content. Field Guide may request YouTube video titles or thumbnails to display a video link. Video playback opens in YouTube or your browser. External services receive the network information needed for those requests and apply their own privacy policies.

Relevant providers include Pinball Map, Google and YouTube, and, for the original PinProf app's optional services, Supabase and PostHog.

6. Storage, exports, and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it in the app, clear app data where supported, or uninstall the app. Your operating system may include some app data in a device backup according to its settings. Field Guide's Android app disables app backup.

When you export or share a field photo, audio note, or other file, the destination you choose receives that file and its included metadata. Copies saved to Photos, Files, another app, a cloud drive, or a backup are managed separately and are not removed by deleting the original field note or uninstalling the app.

For account, synced-data, or server-data deletion questions, contact the email below. Some server cache records are not tied to an identifiable account, so we may need details of the lookup to find the relevant record. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary personal information.

7. Advertising and sharing

We do not sell user data or use third-party advertising SDKs in the apps. We do not track you across apps or websites for targeted advertising.

Information is processed by hosting and content providers and, when you use the relevant features, Pinball Map, YouTube, Supabase, or PostHog. We do not provide user data to advertisers or data brokers. Information may also be disclosed when legally required.

8. Your choices

  • Control camera, photo, location, and microphone access in device settings.
  • Use Field Guide without location access or audio recording.
  • Delete local notes and manage exported copies separately.
  • Turn off analytics in the original PinProf app and choose whether to use Cloud Sync.
  • Choose whether to open external links or share files.
  • Contact us about access or deletion of information held by our services.

9. Children's privacy

The apps are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe such information has been provided so we can address it.

10. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect app operation and content delivery. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
